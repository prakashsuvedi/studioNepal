## Hugging Face startup error fixed

The root cause was exactly what the log reported: `server.js` imports `./mediaJobRoutes`, but that file was not present in the deployed Space. The complete require chain also needed the previously missing `azureClient.js` compatibility module.

I added `azureClient.js`, retained the new Azure gpt-image-1.5 and Sora routes, and verified that the full CommonJS require chain resolves successfully under Node 18.

```text
node --check mediaJobRoutes.js

```
